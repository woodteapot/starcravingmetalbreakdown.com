The legend

They only play where the van dies

Somewhere on Highway 61, an alternator gave up in the dark. Four strangers had been sharing a ride in a retired church van called The Breakdown, and there was nothing to do but plug in. A generator, a slide guitar, a drum machine with a grudge. By sunrise the tow truck driver had cried twice.

That night they looked up, saw more stars than any of them had ever seen, and wanted all of them. They have been driving toward darker skies ever since. That hunger is the star craving. The breakdown is everything else: the riff, the drop, the engine, and on a good night the audience.

  1. No bookings. The show happens wherever the van stops running.
  2. Every set opens with a twelve-bar blues and ends at 174 beats per minute.
  3. The drop waits until the sky is dark enough to deserve it.
  4. Whoever fixes the van gets a song. No exceptions, and three tow truck drivers have royalties.

The four

Who is in the van

Mercy Voltage

Vocals, harmonica

Raised in a Delta church choir and thrown out of it for singing louder than the organ. Mercy sings through a CB radio mic wired to a guitar amp, so every truck for three miles hears the chorus.

Carries: a harmonica in every key but E. Mercy says E has to be earned.

Deacon Rust

Seven-string, slide guitar

Tunes low enough to loosen fillings and plays slide with a spark plug socket. Deacon has rebuilt the van's engine eleven times and claims to have improved it twice.

Carries: the dead alternator from the first night, bolted to the guitar case.

Kilo Hertz

Modular synths, sub-bass

Left a Reno casino with a slot machine's circuit boards and no explanation. Kilo's rig runs off the van battery, which is why the headlights dim on every drop.

Carries: a patch cable said to be longer than Nevada.

Axle Tempo

Drums, machines, mechanic

Keeps time on a kit of tractor parts and one real snare. Axle counts in every song with a torque wrench and has never been late, even in the three states where the clocks disagree.

Carries: a logbook of every breakdown, which is the only set list the band has.

Fan club

The Roadside Assistance

The fan club has no dues, no officers and no mailing list. It has one rule: if you see a van broken down under a good sky, pull over and ask what key it's in.

Member card

Roadside Assistance

  • Rank: Jumper Cable, Tow Hook or Head Gasket
  • Benefits: one song, if you fix the van
  • Secret greeting: "How's the alternator?"
  • Correct reply: "Dying, thanks."

To join: find the darkest sky you can reach, turn your headlights off, and listen for 60 seconds. That's the whole ceremony. You're in.
